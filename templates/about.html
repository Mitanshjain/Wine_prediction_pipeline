About WineIQ

How the ML model works under the hood.

🤖 The Model

WineIQ uses a Random Forest Regressor wrapped in a Scikit-learn Pipeline with StandardScaler preprocessing.

Algorithm: Random Forest (200 trees, max depth 10)
Dataset: 1,143 red wine samples (WineQT)
Train/Test Split: 80% / 20%
MAE: ~0.42 quality points
Quality Scale: 3 (Poor) → 8 (Excellent)

Why Random Forest? Wine quality depends on complex, non-linear interactions between chemical properties — RF captures these far better than Linear Regression.
